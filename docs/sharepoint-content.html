Our Mission

T.O.O.L.S Inc provides comprehensive support and resources for justice-involved individuals. Through programs, education, and community partnerships, we help people rebuild their lives and achieve lasting success.

Programs & Services

💼

Job Readiness Training

Resume building, interview preparation, and job placement assistance to help you succeed in the workforce.

📚

Educational Resources

GED preparation, vocational training, and skills development courses designed for your success.

👥

Case Management

Personalized support with dedicated case managers who track your progress and coordinate services.

🤝

Community Support

Housing assistance, mental health support, and family reunification services.
