## 🎵 Music & Audio

### Music Requirements

**Primary Track** (0:00-2:00):
- **Style**: Cinematic, inspirational, uplifting
- **Mood**: Contemplative → hopeful → triumphant
- **Instruments**: Piano, strings, subtle electronic elements
- **BPM**: 80-110
- **Key Changes**: Minor → Major around 0:30 mark

**Recommended Tracks**:
- Artlist: "New Beginning", "Hope and Inspiration", "Rise Up"
- Epidemic Sound: "Uplifting Corporate", "Inspiring Cinematic"
- AudioJungle: Search "inspirational corporate"

**Budget Options**:
- Free: YouTube Audio Library, Free Music Archive
- Paid: Artlist ($15/month unlimited), Epidemic Sound ($15/month), AudioJungle ($20-40 per track)

### Voiceover Specifications

**Talent Profile**:
- **Gender**: Male or Female (warm, authoritative voice)
- **Age Range**: 35-50 (mature, trustworthy)
- **Accent**: Neutral American or regional as appropriate
- **Tone**: Conversational yet professional, empathetic

**Recording Specs**:
- Format: WAV, 48kHz, 24-bit
- Mono channel
- No background noise or room tone
- -6dB to -3dB peak levels (leaves headroom for mixing)

**Where to Find Voiceover Talent**:
- Fiverr.com ($50-150 for 90-120s)
- Voices.com (professional talent, $200-500)
- Upwork (project-based, $100-300)
- Local talent (check community theater, radio stations)

### Sound Effects Needed

- [ ] UI click/interaction sounds (subtle)
- [ ] Whoosh for transitions
- [ ] Digital/tech sounds for feature highlights
- [ ] Subtle ambient sounds for B-roll
- **Source**: Freesound.org, Epidemic Sound SFX library

## 🎞️ Technical Specifications

### Editing Software

**Recommended**:
- **Adobe Premiere Pro** - Industry standard, great integration with After Effects
- **DaVinci Resolve** - Free version is powerful, excellent color grading
- **Final Cut Pro** - Mac users, optimized performance

### Project Settings

**Sequence/Timeline**:
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- Frame Rate: 30fps or 24fps (cinematic)
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Codec: ProRes 422 HQ (editing), H.264 (export)

### Export Settings

**Master File** (Archive):
- Format: ProRes 422 HQ or DNxHD
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame Rate: Match timeline (30fps or 24fps)

**YouTube/Website**:
- Format: H.264/MP4
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame Rate: 30fps
- Bitrate: 15-20 Mbps (VBR, 2-pass)
- Audio: AAC, 320kbps, 48kHz

**Social Media - Square (1:1)**:
- Resolution: 1080x1080
- Same codec as above
- Cropping: Center cut with manual adjustments

**Social Media - Vertical (9:16)**:
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Same codec as above
- Reframing: Focus on key elements, add extra graphics

**Captions File**:
- Format: SRT or VTT
- Sync to audio timeline
- Include on-screen text

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### DIY Production (Weekend Project)

**Saturday Morning** (3-4 hours):
1. Record screen captures of dashboard and portals
2. Download stock footage from Pexels/Pixabay
3. Record voiceover (use Audacity or GarageBand)

**Saturday Afternoon** (3-4 hours):
4. Create basic logo animation in After Effects or Canva
5. Generate QR codes
6. Organize all assets in project folder

**Sunday** (6-8 hours):
7. Import to editing software
8. Rough cut following script timing
9. Add music and sound effects
10. Create text overlays and captions
11. Color correction and grading
12. Export master file and review

**Monday** (2-3 hours):
13. Make revisions
14. Export platform-specific versions
15. Upload to YouTube/LinkedIn/website

**Total Time**: 14-19 hours over 3 days
