# T.O.O.L.S Inc Launch Video Script

## Video Specifications
- **Duration**: 90-120 seconds
- **Format**: 16:9 (1920x1080)
- **Style**: Professional, hopeful, human-centered
- **Tone**: Empowering, inspiring, solution-focused
- **Target Audience**: Justice-involved individuals, case managers, community partners, potential funders

---

## Scene Breakdown

### SCENE 1: The Challenge (0:00 - 0:15)
**Duration**: 15 seconds

**Visual Direction**:
- Open on slow-motion footage of urban environments at dawn
- Statistics appear as text overlays with subtle animations
- Transition through images of closed doors, barriers, "No Hiring" signs
- Fade to black with last statistic

**Voiceover**:
> "Every year, over 600,000 people return home from incarceration. Within three years, two-thirds will face re-arrest. Why? Because the barriers they face are real. Employment. Housing. Education. Support."

**Music**: 
- Contemplative piano, building slowly
- Subtle tension in strings

**Text Overlays**:
- "600,000+ individuals return annually"
- "67% re-arrest rate within 3 years"
- "90% face employment barriers"

### Alternative Version Recommendations

**60-Second Version** (Social Media):
- Condense Scenes 1-7 into tighter edits
- Focus on key value propositions
- Use punchier text overlays
- Format: 1:1 (square) or 9:16 (vertical) for Instagram/TikTok

**30-Second Teaser**:
- Scene 1 (5s): The problem
- Scene 2 (10s): The solution reveal
- Scene 4 (10s): Human impact
- Scene 7 (5s): CTA

**2-Minute Extended Version** (Website/Presentations):
- Add testimonials (30s)
- Show detailed feature demonstrations (45s)

## Budget Considerations

### DIY Production (Budget: $500-1,500)
- Use existing screen recordings
- Stock footage from Pexels, Pixabay (free)
- Stock music from Artlist ($15/month)
- Self-recorded voiceover or Fiverr talent ($50-150)
- Edit in DaVinci Resolve (free)

### Semi-Professional (Budget: $2,500-5,000)
- Hire videographer for B-roll (1 day shoot)
- Professional voiceover talent
- Premium stock footage and music
- Motion graphics designer for animations
- Professional editor

### Full Production (Budget: $10,000-25,000)
- Full production crew
- Original score
- Multiple shoot locations
- Professional actors/testimonials
- Animation studio for graphics
- Color grading and post-production

## Accessibility Requirements

**Closed Captions**:
- Full transcription of voiceover
- On-screen text also included in captions
- Multiple language options (Spanish, French)

**Audio Description**:
- Narrative description of visual elements
- Available as separate audio track

**Color Contrast**:
- Ensure text overlays meet WCAG AA standards
- High contrast against backgrounds
- Avoid flashing or rapidly changing imagery
